# Stick Battle

Stick Battle is a small browser game built with HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript. Play as an AOL stick figure and battle a lineup of familiar computer icons, including Firefox, Clippy, the MSN butterfly, and a final cursor boss.

## Gameplay

- Click **Attack** to trade attacks with your opponent. Damage varies from turn to turn.
- Defeat opponents to earn score and advance through the battles.
- Open the store between battles to buy upgrades: Firewall improves defense, Minesweeper improves attack, Battery restores health, and Calculator increases score rewards.
- Track your damage, defeated icons, and upgrades in the final stats screen.

## Run the game

No installation or build step is needed. Open `index-stick.html` in a web browser to play.
